# 📊 Population vs Data

## 📌 Introduction

In statistics, understanding the difference between **Population** and **Data** is very important. These two terms form the foundation of statistical analysis.

---

## 1️⃣ Population

### 🔹 Definition

A **Population** is the complete set of individuals, items, or observations that we want to study.

### 🔹 Key Points

- It includes **all members** of a group.
- It can be large or small.
- It may be finite or infinite.

### 🔹 Example

- All students in a college.
- All voters in a country.
- All products manufactured in a factory.

---

## 2️⃣ Data

### 🔹 Definition

**Data** refers to the collected information or observations about individuals or items.

### 🔹 Key Points

- Data can be collected from a **population or sample**.
- It can be **qualitative (categorical)** or **quantitative (numerical)**.
- It is used for analysis and decision-making.

### 🔹 Example

- Marks obtained by students.
- Age of employees.
- Survey responses.

---

## 📌 Difference Between Population and Data

| Feature | Population                 | Data                    |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Meaning | Entire group being studied | Information collected   |
| Size    | Usually large              | Can be small or large   |
| Purpose | Target of study            | Used for analysis       |
| Example | All students in a class    | Marks of those students |

---

## ✅ Conclusion

A **Population** is the complete group we want to study, while **Data** is the information collected from that group. Understanding both concepts is essential for accurate statistical analysis.
